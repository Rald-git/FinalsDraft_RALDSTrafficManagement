INCLUSIVE MOBILITY

Streets designed for all people

Inclusive mobility recognizes that people have different ages, abilities, resources, and travel needs.

Features of inclusive streets

  • Continuous, unobstructed sidewalks
  • Ramps and curb transitions where needed
  • Safe crossings with enough crossing time
  • Clear signs and understandable information
  • Accessible public transport stops and vehicles
  • Seating, shade, and lighting where appropriate

Good street design considers persons with disabilities, older adults, children, caregivers, and everyone who travels on foot.
