TRAFFIC SIGNS

Recognize the message on the road

Traffic signs help regulate movement, warn people about hazards, and guide road users to destinations.

Regulatory signs

Communicate rules and restrictions that road users are required to follow, such as stop, no entry, or speed restrictions.

Warning signs

Alert road users to conditions or hazards ahead, such as curves, intersections, or pedestrian activity.

Guide signs

Provide useful information about routes, destinations, facilities, and directions.
