PUBLIC TRANSPORT

Connect people to opportunities

Public transport plays an important role in accessible, efficient, and sustainable urban mobility.

What makes a better journey?

  • Safe and convenient stops and terminals
  • Clear route and fare information
  • Accessible boarding and passenger facilities
  • Reliable service and responsible driving
  • Well-connected walking and cycling routes

Passengers can help by queuing safely, keeping aisles clear, respecting priority seating, and following operator instructions.
