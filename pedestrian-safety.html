PEDESTRIAN SAFETY

Walking should be safe for everyone

Safe walking environments need continuous footpaths, visible crossings, good lighting, and respectful driving.

For people walking

  • Use sidewalks and designated crossings where available.
  • Pause and check traffic in every direction before crossing.
  • Stay alert and avoid looking at a phone while crossing.
  • At night, choose well-lit routes and make yourself visible.

For drivers

Slow down near crossings, schools, terminals, and places where people walk. Yield as required and never block pedestrian paths.
