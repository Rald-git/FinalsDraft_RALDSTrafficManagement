TRAFFIC RULES

Rules make shared roads safer

Follow official traffic signs, signals, road markings, and instructions from authorized personnel.

Obey signals

Approach intersections carefully and follow traffic lights and authorized traffic directions.

Respect speed limits

Drive at a lawful and safe speed for the road, weather, visibility, and surrounding activity.

Keep safe distance

Leave enough space to react and stop safely when traffic conditions change.

Never drive impaired

Do not drive after drinking alcohol or using impairing substances.

This page is for general education and is not a substitute for current Philippine laws, official regulations, or legal advice.
