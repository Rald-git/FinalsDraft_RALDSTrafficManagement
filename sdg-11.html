UNITED NATIONS SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS

SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

Make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable.

Why road safety belongs here

People need safe ways to move around their communities. Road safety, reliable public transport, accessible footpaths, and thoughtful street planning all contribute to more livable places.

This project connects traffic education with the wider goal of creating communities where people can travel safely and participate in daily life.
