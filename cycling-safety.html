CYCLING SAFETY

Share the road with care

Safer cycling requires predictable behaviour, appropriate equipment, and road environments that protect vulnerable users.

Practical cycling tips

  • Wear a properly fitted helmet where appropriate and required.
  • Use lights and reflective items when visibility is low.
  • Check brakes, tires, and other bicycle parts regularly.
  • Signal intentions and ride predictably.
  • Use designated bicycle facilities when available.
  • Drivers should pass only when it is safe and leave sufficient space.
