ROAD MARKINGS

Understand the lines beneath your wheels

Road markings help organize traffic and make movements more predictable.

Common markings

  • Lane lines: separate traffic lanes and help guide positioning.
  • Stop lines: show where vehicles should stop at an intersection or crossing.
  • Pedestrian crossings: identify places where people may cross.
  • Directional arrows: indicate permitted or expected travel directions.
  • Edge lines: help define the edge of the travelled way.

Always observe signs, signals, and local traffic instructions alongside pavement markings.
