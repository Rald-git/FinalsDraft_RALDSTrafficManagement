ROAD SAFETY

Make every trip a safer trip

Road safety depends on careful decisions, safe speeds, and respect among all road users.

Everyday safety habits

  • Pay attention and avoid distractions while driving or crossing.
  • Use seat belts and appropriate child restraints.
  • Follow posted speed limits and adjust to road conditions.
  • Never drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Give pedestrians and cyclists adequate space.
  • Keep vehicles maintained and roadworthy.
