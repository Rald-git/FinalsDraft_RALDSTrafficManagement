SCHOOL ZONE SAFETY

Safe routes help learners thrive

Children need extra protection around schools, crossings, drop-off areas, and nearby streets.

School-zone checklist

  • Reduce speed and follow school-zone signs.
  • Stop only in designated loading and unloading areas.
  • Keep crossings and sidewalks clear.
  • Teach learners to cross with care and follow crossing staff.
  • Plan safe walking routes and supervised pick-up points.

Schools, families, barangays, and transport providers can coordinate to identify hazards and improve routes.
