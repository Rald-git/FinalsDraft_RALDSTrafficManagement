EMERGENCY RESPONSE

Protect life and prevent further harm

Road incidents can be stressful. Prioritize safety, seek qualified help, and follow instructions from emergency responders.

If you witness a road incident

  1. Move to a safe place and avoid standing in active traffic.
  2. Contact the appropriate local emergency service or authorities.
  3. Give clear information about the location and nature of the incident.
  4. Do not move an injured person unless there is immediate danger.
  5. Follow instructions from trained responders.

Emergency contact numbers and procedures may vary by location. Confirm current contacts with your local government and official emergency services.
